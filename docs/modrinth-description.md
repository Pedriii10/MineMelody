**Turn the Minecraft soundtrack into your own playlist.** No more 20 minutes of silence between songs while you mine for hours.

## ✨ Features

### 🎵 Your music, your way
- **Build playlists** from every track in the game: Overworld, Nether, End, underwater, creative and music discs.
- **In order, shuffle or repeat one.** Shuffle never repeats a track until all of them have played.
- **Configurable gap** between tracks: from 0 seconds to 1 minute (vanilla waits 5–20 minutes!).
- **▶ Preview any track** instantly before adding it.

### 📚 Multiple playlists
- Create as many named playlists as you want ("Mining", "Building", "Chill"...).
- **Bind a playlist to a dimension**: your Nether playlist starts automatically when you go through the portal.

### 💿 Survival discs
- In survival, music discs only play once you have **obtained them** in that world. Find a disc, unlock its song.
- Can be turned off. In creative, everything is available.

### 📻 Now playing banner
- Shows title, artist and icon when a track starts — or **always on screen** with a progress bar.
- Choose the corner and the size (50%–200%), with a **live preview**.

### 🔎 Quality of life
- Search box (ignores case and accents), **★ favorites**.
- **Export/import** playlists as text to share them with friends.
- Track durations and elapsed time.
- Optional keys: next track, next playlist, pause/resume, music volume up/down.
- English and Spanish translations.

## 🕹️ How to use
- **Options → Music & Sounds → MineMelody...**, or press **M** in game.
- Add tracks with double click, turn **Playlist: ON**, and enjoy.
- Extra keys are unbound by default: set them in **Options → Controls → MineMelody**.

## 📦 Requirements
- Minecraft **26.3**
- [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/) and [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)

**Client-side only**: works on any server, the server doesn't need it.

## 🧩 Compatibility
Works with resource packs and mods that add music: their tracks show up in the list automatically.
